Privacy Policy

Anuj Vats Video Tool · Effective 15 October 2026 · Home

The Anuj Vats Video Tool ("the tool") is a private tool that I, Anuj Vats, run on my own computer to publish my own videos. Only I use it. This page explains what it accesses and how that information is handled.

What the tool accesses

  • Google (YouTube and Google Drive): permission to upload videos to my own YouTube channel, and to read and save video files in my own Google Drive.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram): permission to see my Facebook Page and my linked Instagram professional account, and to publish posts and videos to them.

The tool only signs in to my own accounts. It doesn't access anyone else's account.

How the information is used

  • The tool uses this access only to edit my videos, prepare their captions and titles, and publish them after I approve them.
  • Videos are processed on my own computer.
  • To draft captions and titles, the tool sends the spoken words of a video (as text) to an AI writing service (Anthropic's Claude). That text is used only to write that post.
  • The tool doesn't sell, rent or share any data for advertising, and has no analytics or tracking.

Storage and security

  • Sign-in tokens and working files are stored only on my own computer, which uses full-disk encryption, in files only my user account can read.
  • Finished videos are saved to my own Google Drive.
  • No passwords or payment details are stored by the tool.

People who appear in my videos

If someone else appears in one of my videos, for example a client sharing their experience, their image and voice are used only to publish that video, and I publish only with their permission. To have a video featuring you removed, email me at the address below.

Google API Services

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data deletion and revoking access

  • To ask for any data related to you to be deleted, email vatscode@gmail.com. I'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Facebook and Instagram access can be revoked in Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above.
