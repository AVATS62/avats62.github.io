About the Anuj Vats Video Tool

The Anuj Vats Video Tool is a private tool I run on my own computer. It helps me prepare my own videos and publish them to my own YouTube channel, my own Facebook Page and my own Instagram account. It isn't offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it does

  • Reads my raw video files from my own Google Drive.
  • Edits them on my computer: trims pauses, cleans up the audio, adds captions and my branding.
  • Drafts titles and captions for me to review. Nothing is published until I approve it.
  • Uploads approved videos to my own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account.
  • Saves the finished videos back to my own Google Drive.
